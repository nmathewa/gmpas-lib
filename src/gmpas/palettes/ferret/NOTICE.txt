Ferret colour palettes
======================

These .spk files are copied byte-for-byte from the NOAA-PMEL Ferret
repository, https://github.com/NOAA-PMEL/Ferret, directory palettes/,
at commit 89f53cb33aab44906d78b508dabb6eae2e6208df.

Ferret is released into the public domain; its licence is reproduced
below. gmpas parses these files at runtime (gmpas.palettes.spk) and
registers them with matplotlib as ferret.<file name>.

Files included:
  default.spk
  rnb2.spk
  rainbow.spk
  light_rainbow.spk
  medium_rainbow.spk
  inverse_rainbow.spk
  light_centered.spk
  white_centered.spk
  centered.spk
  red_blue_centered.spk
  no_green_centered.spk
  blue_orange.spk
  blue_darkred.spk
  brown_blue.spk
  bluescale.spk
  redscale.spk
  greenscale.spk
  grayscale.spk
  land_sea.spk
  terrestrial.spk
  dark_terrestrial.spk
